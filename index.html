How it works

From booking to delivery in four steps

Whether you are a small business moving one load or a large company running a fleet, our network keeps it simple.

01

Post your shipment

Tell us your company, purpose, type of goods, pickup point and destination.

Add weight, quantity, nature of the goods and how fast they must arrive.

02

Choose a partner truck

Pick from verified trucks owned by small and large companies near your pickup city.

Filter by size, type and city. Every owner sets its own daily and per-km rate.

03

Get an instant quote

See the full price up front: lease days, distance, goods handling, insurance and fees.

Express and same-day delivery are priced in too, so there are no surprises.

04

Track from A to B

Every trip gets a tracking ID so you can follow your goods until delivery.

See the truck on the route map, the progress and the estimated arrival time.

Transparent pricing

Know the full cost before you book

Truck owners set their own day and per-km rates. You see every part of the price, and the owner company is paid for the use of its truck.

Lease duration

Daily rate × number of days you need the truck.

Distance

Per-km rate × the distance from Point A to Point B, estimated automatically.

Goods & cover

A handling charge for special goods (e.g. glass, drugs), 2% insurance and a 5% service fee.
